Orbit's adaptations select exact game/edition and supported-platform matches,
exclude ambiguous records, require at least three playtime submissions per style,
and convert documented average playtimes in seconds to rounded minutes.
Platform-specific critic scores are kept separate from scores for other platforms.

The adapted CC BY-SA records are available under CC BY-SA 4.0. That licence
applies to those metadata records, separately from Orbit's application code and
other catalogue fields. Per-record source, licence and snapshot provenance are
retained. No review text, player reviews or individual user submissions are included.
Metacritic and HowLongToBeat are credited as the original score/playtime services;
their inclusion does not imply endorsement of Orbit.
